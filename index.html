About

I'm a senior backend engineer specializing in production Python for AI/LLM systems — retrieval agents, source verification, and the gateway layer that fronts them. Seven-plus years across startups and agencies, with a consistent through-line: Domain-Driven Design, workflow orchestration, and developer-productivity tooling.

Lately that's meant authoring a company-wide Temporal standard and driving a shared workflow platform to production-grade security and reliability — the unglamorous foundation everything else runs on.
