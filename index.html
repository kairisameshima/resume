About

I'm a senior backend engineer specializing in production Python for AI/LLM systems — retrieval agents, source verification, and the gateway layer that fronts them. Seven-plus years across startups and agencies, with a consistent through-line: Domain-Driven Design, workflow orchestration, and developer-productivity tooling.

At Farsight AI I own several services end-to-end and deliberately work the v1→v2 quality bridge — the gap where most startup MVPs die. My bet is that engineering quality is a tooling and standards problem, not a meeting problem: I'd rather encode a pattern into a scaffold, a CI gate, or an agent plugin than write another doc no one reads.

Lately that's meant authoring a company-wide Temporal standard and driving a shared workflow platform to production-grade security and reliability, then co-designing a delegated-authorization service that shipped to production in August 2026 — the unglamorous foundation everything else runs on.

Security · Interactive

The Envelope Bank

Envelope encryption as a miniature bank. A teller locks your letter with a one-time data key, a vault keeps the master key that seals it, and the deposit boxes only ever hold locked boxes. A second act covers delegation grants: A has the bank sign a short-lived slip so B can fetch A's access token without ever holding A's keys. A ledger runs both flows in real WebCrypto.

 Auth · Interactive

The Checkpoint

How an auth gateway validates north/south traffic from users and east/west traffic between services, told as a border crossing. Travelers sign in with PKCE and get a short-lived entry pass; services trade for transit passes signed with a separate key, naming who they act for and the one service they may call. A ledger runs every check with real RS256 keys.
