AI Monitoring Service

Scheduled AI monitoring over financial and web sources — LLM-based story evaluation and deduplication, with notifications on material events — migrated onto durable orchestration and re-architected for the long haul.

PythonTemporalGeminiPostgreSQLClean ArchitectureAWS CDK
S
Situation

Users needed timely, low-noise alerts on material events across financial filings and the web — surfaced and deduplicated by AI. The original service ran on a task-queue architecture that was hard to observe and reason about as the workload grew.

T
Task

Own the service, modernize its orchestration to something durable and observable, and re-architect it so it stays extensible as the domain grows — the v1→v2 bridge.

A
Action

Ran scheduled queries with LLM-based story evaluation and semantic deduplication, dispatching notifications on material events.

  • Led the migration from the task-queue architecture to Temporal across five milestones — orchestrator, reporter, evaluator, notifier, and a real-time filings ingestion pipeline.
  • Re-architected the codebase to Clean Architecture with an async Unit-of-Work pattern, async repositories, and constructor-injected activities — later profiling and reverting to sync where it improved throughput.
  • Built a worker-liveness healthcheck from SDK metrics and wired exception reporting, closing blind spots in health monitoring.
R
Result

A modernized monitoring service on durable Temporal orchestration with a clean, extensible architecture and real observability — the clearest example in my work of taking a functioning v1 and rebuilding it onto foundations that survive growth.
