Agentic Research Agent

An agentic research service that answers financial and open-domain questions by autonomously orchestrating multi-turn tool calls, then streams cited, verifiable answers.

PythonFastAPITemporalClaude / BedrockSSERedis
S
Situation

The product needed to answer complex financial and open-domain questions with cited, trustworthy evidence — which requires autonomous, multi-source retrieval rather than a single LLM call. An early serverless prototype proved the idea but couldn't scale to production reliability or observability.

T
Task

Own the service end-to-end and take it from prototype to a scalable, observable, production-grade architecture — retrieval quality, latency, and deployment included.

A
Action

Built an agentic loop that orchestrates multi-turn tool calls across SEC filings, market-data providers, and web search, streaming cited answers over SSE with character-level citation anchoring and quick/deep LLM modes.

  • Migrated the service from the serverless prototype to a Temporal multi-worker pipeline with per-tool retry policies and large-payload offload.
  • Instrumented per-activity latency, diagnosed a concurrency cliff under parallel load, and tuned worker pools to resolve it — guided by a custom load-test harness.
  • Built a DynamoDB-backed evaluation framework tracking response quality across backend versions, plus CI quality gates and multi-environment deploy pipelines.
R
Result

A production-capable research agent with measured latency, an eval framework that turns quality into a regression-testable metric, and a hardened deployment pipeline — the retrieval engine other verification products build on.
