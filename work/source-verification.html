Source Verification Service

A microservice that extracts factual claims from documents and verifies each against retrieved evidence — producing auditable verdicts with citations.

PythonTemporalClean ArchitectureLLM evalLangfuseAWS CDK
S
Situation

AI-generated and analyst-authored content needs its factual claims checked against real evidence — at scale, and with auditable, defensible verdicts rather than a black-box yes/no. There was no service that did this systematically.

T
Task

Design and build — as sole author — a service that ingests documents, extracts their factual claims, and verifies each claim against retrieved evidence, with quality measurable enough to make ship decisions on.

A
Action

Built the service ground-up on a clean-architecture foundation with a two-phase Temporal workflow — prepare evidence, then verify each claim — producing verified / rejected / uncertain verdicts with citations and explanations.

  • Aligned the verification contract to the companion research-agent's schema and bounded fan-out concurrency to prevent burst overload.
  • Built a golden-set evaluation harness: 63 hand-labeled claims auto-labeled via a frontier model with zero numeric-hallucination failures.
  • Wrote a replay/sweep CLI emitting per-claim latency (p50/p95), agreement-with-golden, cost, and linkable LLM traces — so research-depth variants could be compared on data.
R
Result

A complete, evidence-backed verification service with an evaluation harness that converts "is it accurate enough?" into a measured, repeatable decision — owned end-to-end from the first scaffold commit through infrastructure and CI.
